# pedagogy/02: Scope experiential learning (knowledge → wisdom)

**Status:** open
**Depends on:** — (seam with ai-chat/04)

## Why

"Knowledge to wisdom (experiential learning)" — the teach skill's Wisdom today is *delegated
outward* ("find a community, test your skills in the real world"). This ticket scopes making
experience **first-class in the platform**: real-world assignments, doing the thing,
reporting back, and teach-back — the pyramid's practice/teach-others end (seam: pedagogy/01
owns the frame; ai-chat/04 owns the *conversational* apply-coaching).

## Questions to answer

- The assignment concept: is a real-world task a Lesson variant (an HTML artifact saying "go
  do X, come back") — which the teach skill can author *today* with zero schema — or a new
  entity with its own state (issued → attempted → reflected)? Ponytail says start as a Lesson
  variant; what breaks?
- The report-back loop: the learner did the thing — where does their reflection land so the
  Routine reads it? A [[Response]] to a prompt in the assignment lesson (existing capture!)
  vs. a new capture kind. Does a poor experience trigger remediation (same policy seam as
  rich-media/07)?
- Teach-back: "explain it in your own words / teach it to someone" — evaluated by the AI
  (needs ai-chat substrate) or self-assessed? Is teach-back the natural [[Completion]]
  gate for a concept?
- Communities: still delegate outward (status quo), or do tenants change this — a UPF cohort
  *is* a community; does experiential learning want cohort-shared reflections later
  (explicitly out of v1)?
- Verification honesty: the platform can't verify real-world action — design for
  self-report without pretending otherwise (no fake "verified" badges).
- Naming: **Assignment**? **Field work**? **Practice**? — propose the CONTEXT.md term.

## Out of scope

- The apply-outcomes *conversation* (ai-chat/04).
- Cohorts/multi-learner features.

## Deliverable

The assignment-representation decision (Lesson variant vs entity), the report-back capture
rule, the teach-back evaluation answer, and the proposed term.
