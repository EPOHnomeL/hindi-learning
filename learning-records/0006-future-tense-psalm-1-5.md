# The future tense (stem + -एँगе) taught — Psalm 1:5 read whole

Lesson 8 (`0008-they-will-not-stand.html`) taught the **Hindi future tense**, the
first non-present tense the learner has met. This is the top ZPD step recorded
after L7: Psalm 1:5–6 is the only psalm-text left before the mission's first
waypoint (the whole psalm), and v5 is where the future first appears.

Grounded in handbook **§9.1, printed p.113** (verified by rendering the scanned
spread at high DPI): quoted near word-for-word per NOTES —
- "The future tense is one of the easiest. It consists of a **single word**, with
  **no auxiliary** (unlike the English!)."
- The endings: "**-ऊँगा** for मैं, **-एगा** for all the other singulars; **-ओगे**
  for तुम, and **-एँगे** for all the other plurals. Feminines follow suit, with
  **-ई** as the final vowel." (Irregulars होना/लेना/देना noted but not drilled.)
- `ठहरना` "to stop, stay on" cited from handbook vocabulary **p.115**.

Vehicle: the two futures the verse hands over, **both masculine plural → both take
-एँगे**, which made one clean rule do all the work:
- `ठहरेंगे` = ठहर + -एँगे "(they) will abide" (subject पापी).
- `रह सकेंगे` = रह + सकना + -एँगे "will be able to stand" (subject दुष्ट लोग).

`सकना` ("to be able / can", bare stem + सकना) was introduced **lightly** as a
secondary note, deliberately NOT made the lesson — the teaching anchor stayed the
future ending, which is visible on both सकेंगे and ठहरेंगे. Verse 5 text verified
character-for-character against bible.com 819 (HHBD) and cross-checked against a
second source.

**Can now be assumed:** Jonathan recognises the future on sight (a verb ending in
-ऊँगा / -एगा / -ओगे / -एँगे, fem. -ई), knows it is a single word with no auxiliary,
and can add the plural ending to a stem (the fill quiz targets exactly this:
ठहर → ठहरेंगे). He has met `सकना` "be able to" as stem + सकना. **Psalm 1:1–5 is now
fully parsed, nothing deferred.**

New glossary entries: इस कारण, अदालत, स्थिर, सकना, पापी, धर्मी, मण्डली, ठहरना. New
grammar-reference terms: "Future tense (बोलेगा)" with the §9.1 endings + irregulars,
and "Potential (रह सकना)".
