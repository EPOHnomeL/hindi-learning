# Reads Devanagari fluently; gap is vocabulary + grammar

Jonathan reads the Devanagari script comfortably and aloud — his own statement,
and the reason he can "almost understand" Psalm 1. The bottleneck is **not** the
alphabet, vowel marks, or conjuncts; it is **vocabulary and grammar**.

**Implications for teaching:**
- Skip all script/alphabet instruction. Do not spend lessons on अ आ क ख, mātrās,
  or conjuncts unless he hits a specific decoding snag and asks.
- Start lessons at the level of parsing real Bible verses: word meanings +
  the grammar that binds them.
- Keep transliteration to a **minimum** (his explicit request) — only for
  genuinely tricky pronunciation. Present Devanagari first, always.
- The natural first vehicle is Psalm 1, which he already half-reads.
