# Mission: Hindi

## Why
Jonathan wants to read and understand the Hindi Bible — specifically the BSI OV
(Bible Society of India, "Old Version" / पवित्र बाइबल) — in the original
Devanagari script, unaided. The first concrete waypoint is fully understanding
Psalm 1 (भजन संहिता 1), which he can already _almost_ read. From there, to read
Scripture devotionally in Hindi as an ongoing, lifelong practice.

## Success looks like
- Read aloud and understand every word of Psalm 1 in the BSI OV, with no crutch.
- Read fluently from the Devanagari script (no transliteration needed).
- Work through Bible passages independently, parsing grammar as they go.
- Build a growing personal vocabulary anchored in Scripture he reads regularly.
- Sustain a steady, lifelong Hindi-reading habit grounded in the Bible.

## Constraints
- Learning is grounded in two anchored resources: the handbook
  (Snell & Weightman, *Teach Yourself Hindi*) and the BSI OV Hindi Bible.
- Devotional/biblical register is the priority — not conversational tourist Hindi.
- Lessons should be short, frequent, and give a tangible win each time.

## Out of scope
- Conversational/spoken fluency as a primary goal (it will come second to reading).
- Other Hindi Bible versions unless useful for contrast with the BSI OV.
- Formal Sanskrit, Urdu script (Nastaliq), or other Indian languages.
