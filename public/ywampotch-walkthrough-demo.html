Lesson 1 · Hearing God's Voice

Learning to Listen

Scripture: John 16:13 One skill: a daily listening practice ~10 min

Each truly reborn Christian has one question. How can I grow in my walk with God? God has given us the Holy Spirit in the new covenant. When we want to grow in our relationship with the Holy Spirit it begins with one skill: quieting down and listening.

John 16:13 · The Spirit of Truth
"When the Spirit of truth comes, he will guide you into all the truth… and he will declare to you the things that are to come."

1 You were made to hear

Hearing God is not a gift for a spiritual elite. It flows from the relationship you already have: if you have received the Holy Spirit, He is already inside you and can speak to you.

2 The practice — seven steps

  1. Find a quiet place in the house.
  2. Repent of all known sins.
  3. Rebuke all evil — in Jesus' name, ask any other voice to be silent.
  4. Focus on what God wants to let you know, as you clear your mind.
  5. Allow the Holy Spirit to speak — and believe what you hear.
  6. Test it according to scripture.
  7. Act on what you believe from God.

3 The test that keeps you safe

Because we're learning, we will sometimes get it wrong. Scripture tells us to test what we hear rather than take everything as from the Holy Spirit — or trust nothing.

5 Check yourself
