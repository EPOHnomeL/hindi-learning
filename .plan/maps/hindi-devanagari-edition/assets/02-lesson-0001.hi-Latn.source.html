LESSON 1 • PARMESHWAR KI AWAAZ KO SUNNA

Sunna Seekhana

 Har ek sachche naye janam paye huye Masihi ka ek sawal hota hai. Main Parmeshwar ke saath chalne mein kaise aage badhun ? Parmeshwar ne nayi vacha (new covenant) mein humein Pavitra Atma diya hai.  Jab hum Pavitra Atma ke saath apne rishte ko badhna chahte hain toh iski shuruat ek kaushal (skill) se hoti hai: shant hona aur sunna.

Zariya : Yuhanna (John) 16:13 Ek kaushal : Rozana sunne ka abhyas ~10 min

  Yeh aapki pehli shiksha hai, isliye iska sanchep (recap) apka uddeshya (mission) hai: Pavitra Atma ke saath ek jeevit rishte mein badhna, Unke vardanom mein badhna, aur Parmeshwar ki awaaz ko sunna. Yeh teeno ke badhne ka ek hi aadhar (root) hai — sunne ki aadat. Hum wahin se shuru karte hain.


Aapko Parmeshwar ki awaaz ko sunne ke liye koi mehnat karne ya durr swarg  tak pahunchne ki zarurat nahi hai. Pavitra Atma pehle se hi aap ke andar rehta hai, aur woh aapse baat karna chahta hai. Aaj ki shiksha ek bohot hi mahatvapurna aur gehri jeevan ki aadat par kendrit hai, jise aap apna sakte hain: Shant hokar baithna aur Unki awaaz sunne ka tareeqa —  saath hi ek asaan parakh (test) , jo seekhne ke waqt aapko surakshit rakhti hai. Kuch vishvasi yeh nahi mante ki aap Pavitra Atma ki awaaz sun sakte hain. Lekin aaiye hum vachan mein dekhein.

 

Yuhanna (John) 16:13 Satya ka atma

"Parantu jab wo, arthath Satya ka atma ayega toh wo tumhe sab Satya ka marg dikhayega,  kyunki wo apni taraf se na kahega, parantu jo kuch sunega wahi karega, aur aanewali baatein tumhe batayega."

Yeshu ka apna vayda: Atma ka kaam marg-darshan karna aur bolna hai. Unhe sunna koi dharna nahi hai — yahi karne ke liye toh woh aaye thae.

1 Aap sunne ke liye banaye gaye hain

Parmeshwar ko sunna sirf kuch khaas (elite) adhyatmik logon ke liye vardan nahi hai. Yeh us rishte se behta hai jo aapka pehle se hi hai: agar aapne Pavitra Atma ko sweekar kiya hai, toh Woh pehle se hi aapke andar hai aur aap se baat kar sakta hai. Swargiya Pitah ko khushi hoti hai apni atma usse dene mein jo bus unn se mangta hai.

Luka (luke) 11:13
"So jab tum bure hokar apne bachon ko achi vastuein (good gifts) dena jaante ho, Toh swargiya Pita apne maangne walon ko Pavitra Atma kyun na dega!"

Yeh pathyakram (course) saaf taur par kehta hai: "Aap Pavitra Atma ki awaaz kisi bhi samay aur kisi bhi jagah sun sakte hain, lekin iske liye ek aise kaan hone ki zaroorat hai jo sunna chahta ho aur jo har din niyamit roop se Pavitra Atma par dhyan kendrit karta ho." Samasya yeh nahi ki Parmeshwar ko bolne ki itchaa nahi hai — balki samasya yeh hai ki hum sunne ke liye rukte nahi hain.

Agar aapko yakeen nahi hai ki Parmeshwar ka astitva hai, ya aap ek Masihi nahi hain toh aap nichae likhe huye vachan par vichar kar sakte hain:

Yuhanna (John) 3:16

"Kyunki Parmeshwar ne jagat se aisa prem kiya ki usne apna eklauta Putra de diya, taaki jo koi uss par vishwas kare, woh nash na ho, parantu anant jeevan paye". 

Agar aapko vishwas hai ki yehi sach ho sakta hai toh aap is romanchak yatra mein shamil hone ke liye mere saath yeh prathna kar sakte hain.

He Parmeshwar jisne iss srishti ki rachna ki hai; yadi aapne Yeshu ko apne Putra ke roop mein dharti par bheja aur yeshu ne sabhi ke liye, aur mere liye bhi Krus par apne praan diye, toh kripya mujhe Pavitra Atma dein aur Unhe mujhse baat karne dein.

Agar aapne yeh prathna ki hai toh aapka naya Janam ho gya hai. Aapke saath abhi kya hua hai yeh samajhne ke liye aap Yuhanna (John) 3 :1-21 padh sakte hain. 

Aap shayad andaruni anand aur shanti ka anubhav karenge lekin agar aapko kuch bhi mehsoos na ho, toh bhi aap ab yeh anubhav karne lagenge ki Parmeshwar jeevit hai!

Source: Wikus Vorster, Holy Spirit Course ("Walking in Power"), chapter Guidance of the Holy Spirit, p. 23.

2 Abhyas — Parmeshwar ki awaaz sunne ke saat kadam

Yahan aapke course se juda ek thos abhyas diya gaya hai. Isse jaan-bujhkar chota rakha gaya hai: isse sirf padhein nahi, balki karke dekhein. Aaj iske liye kuch minute alag se nikalein.

Pavitra Atma ko kaise sunein

  1. Ghar mein ek shant jagah khojein.
  2. Apne sabhi paapon se jo apko pata hai, pashchatap (repent) karein.
  3. Har ek buri atma ko dant lagaein— Yeshu ke naam mein, kisi bhi dusri awaaz ko chup rehne ke liye kahein.
  4. Jab aap apne mann ko saaf kar rahe hain, toh iss waqt uss baat par dhyan lgaein ki Parmeshwar aapko kya batana chahta hai.
  5. Pavitra Atma ko bolne ya ehsaas dilane dein — aur jo aap sunte hain uss baat par vishwas karein.
  6. Bible ke vachan ke anusar uski jaanch karein.
  7. Uss baat par amal karein, jo apko vishwas hai ki ye Parmeshwar ki taraf se hai aur Bible ke vachan ke anusar hai.
Sabse zaruri kadam 5 hai — "jo aap sunte hain us par vishvas karein." Zyadatar naye log kuch dheemi awaaz sunte hain, phir turant usse "yeh sirf mera vaham hai" kehkar nazar-andaz kar dete hain. Sunna seekhne ka matlab hai ki uss ehsaas ko bohot jaldi na chhorna. Aap uski jaanch karte hain (kadam 6); aap isse pehle se hi raddh nahi karte.

3 Woh jaanch jo aapko surakshit rakhti hai

Sabse pehle hum apne andar dhyan kendrit karne ke liye shant hote hain. Phir hum Parmeshwar ke samne shuddh hone ke liye apne sabhi jaane-pehchane paapon se pashchatap karte hain. Ismein 30 seconds se zyada samay nahi lagna chahiye. Phir hum unn sabhi buri awaazon ko dantte hain jo humse baat kar sakti hain, taaki hum shaitani shor se mukt hokar shanti paa sakein. Ab aap Pavitra Atma se apne sawal pooch sakte hain.

Kyunki hum seekh rahe hain, isliye kabhi-kabhi humse galti ho sakti hai — hum apne vicharon ya bhavnaon ko hi Parmeshwar ki awaaz samajh sakte hain . Yeh samanya baat hai, aur iss se bachne ke liye upay bhi hai. Bible ka vachan humein sikhata hai ki jo kuch hum sunte hain, usse parkhein; na ki har baat ko Pavitra Atma ki taraf se maan lein ya phir kisi bhi baat par vishwas hi na karein :

1 yuhanaa (John) 4:1–2
"Hey priyo, har ek atma par vishwas na karo, varan atmaaon ko parkho ki woh Parmeshwar ki taraf se hain ki nahi… Parmeshwar ke Atma ko tum is tarah se pehchan sakte ho: 
jo koi atma yeh maan leti hai ki Yeshu Masih shareer mein hokar aaya hai, woh Parmeshwar ki taraf se hai."

Teen jaldi se ki huyi jaanch jo "mujhe lagta hai maine kuch suna" ko vishvas mein badal deti hain:

1 · Kya yeh Yeshu ka aadar karta hai? → 2 · Kya yeh Bible ke vachan se mel khata hai? → 3 · Kya ismein Unki shanti hai?

Atma kabhi bhi likhe huye Vachan ke khilaaf baat nahi karti, aur jaisa ki pathyakram ki notes batati hain, unki awaaz "shanti ki baat karti hai." Dosh lagana, ghabrahat, aur dabaav kisi dusri awaaz ke lakshan hain; Pavitra Atma narmi se samjhati hai aur peeche shanti chorr jati hai. Aur ek naye vishwasi ke liye achchi khabar yeh hai:

Holy Spirit Course · p. 23

"Jitna zyada aap Pavitra Atma ko sunne ki koshish karenge, utna hi zyada behtar tarike se aap fark samajh payenge, ki kya aapke mann aur bhavna se hai, aur kya Pavitra Atma se hai."

Toh yeh kaushal (skill) abhyas karne se behtar hota jata hai. Galat hone ke Darr se — aur is wajah se, kabhi na sunne se — aage na badh pana nishchit hai.

4 Woh anek tareekon se bolta hai

Sirf ek sunayi dene wali awaaz ka intezar na karein. Aapke pathyakram (course) mein unn kai tareekon ka zikr hai jinka upayog Parmeshwar karte hain — un sabhi ke zariye unn se milne ki umeed rakhein:

Unki awaaz shanti vichar sapne darshan Koi prerna (prompting) dusre log  Paristithi (circumstances) Bible ke vachan

Source: Vorster, Holy Spirit Course, Guidance of the Holy Spirit, "Ways God can speak", p. 24.

5 Khud ko jaanchein

Tatkal pratikriya (feedback) — kisi jawab par click karein.

1. Koi awaaz parmeshwar ki hai ya nahi, yeh bible ke anusar kaise parkhein ?
2. Apne mann ko shant karne ke baad, abhyas mein aage kya karne ko kaha gya hai?
3. Samay ke saath Pavitra Atma ko sunna aasan kyun ho jata hai?
4. Khali jagah bharein: Pavitra Atma ki awaaz "____ mein bolti hai." (ek shabd)

6 Aapki jeet

Ab aapke paas do aisi cheezein hain jo aaj subah aapke paas nahi thi: Pavitra Atma ki awaaz ko baithkar sunne ke liye ek abhyas jise aap baar baar kar sakte hain, aur sikhte waqt aapko surakshit rakhne ke liye ek teen-bhaag ki jaanch (test).

Apne agle sabak se pehle ise ek baar karein
Aaj paanch minute nikalein
Inn Chhah kadamon ko ek baar karein. Shant jagah, aankhein band, buri atma ko dant lagaein, dhyan kendrit karein, sunein, aur jo pehla ehsaas aapko mile usse likh lein.
Isse Teen jaanchon ke aadhar par parakhein
Kya yeh Yeshu ko aadar deta hai? Kya yeh Bible ke vachan ke anusar hai? Kya iss se shanti milti hai? Agar haan — toh aagyakari hokar ek kadam badhaein.

Lesson 2 ke liye tayyar

Agla sawal jo shuruat karne waale log aam taur par puchte hain, wah yeh hai: "Main Pavitra Atma ki awaaz aur apne vicharon aur bhavnaon ke bich kaise antar karoon?" Hum seedhe issi vishay par baat karenge — yaani unn sanketon par jo pavitra atma ki prerna ko aapke mann se alag karte hain — aur yeh, aaj seekhe gaye jaanch (test) par hi aadharit hoga.

Main aapka shikshak hoon — mujhse kuch bhi puchein. Kya aapko pakka nahi pata ki jo ehsaas aapko hua, wah sach mein Unka hi tha? Mere paas aaiye aur hum milkar iski jaanch karenge. Kya aap parmeshwar ki awaaz sunne ke baare mein aur bhi Bible ke vachan janna chahte hain, ya abhyas ka koi aisa tarika chahte hain jise aap aankhein band karne ke bajaye tahalte huye bhi aazma sakein? Bas chat mein puchein.
