# Set the evidence bar

> `/wayfinder .plan/maps/ui-overhaul/tickets/12-set-evidence-bar.md`

## Question

How much evidence is enough before ticket 13 can name and rank the clunky flows? The
number was deliberately deferred on 2026-08-02 because nobody knew whether a week of
instrumentation would yield 5 sessions or 50.

## Ruled out

**Ruled out 2026-08-27 as ceremony, and the bar written straight into ticket 13.**

The volume already decides it. At around ten lifetime sales there is exactly one
defensible bar, and it needs no meeting to arrive at: watch every recorded session,
because there are few enough to watch. A percentage or a confidence level is
meaningless at this volume, and "N thousand events" would never be met. A whole
grilling ticket to reach a conclusion its own Question already contains is a session
spent on nothing.

The two parts worth keeping moved into ticket 13's Done-when in the same edit:

- **The bar**: every recorded session in the two weeks after ticket 10 goes live,
  watched individually. Funnels are navigation into replays, never statistics.
- **The stop rule**: 13 proceeds on whatever exists at the end of that window, so a
  thin trickle of traffic cannot stall the map. This was the real value of 12, which
  existed as a mitigation against drift, and a date in 13 mitigates it better than an
  unclaimed ticket does.

Also kept, as a rule inside 13 rather than a decision here: a self-walkthrough on a
real phone is admitted alongside real sessions, and every finding is labelled
observed or inferred. An untagged guess quietly becoming an observation was the risk
grilling flagged, and labelling is the whole fix.

Ticket 13 is now `blocked_by: [08, 10]` and no longer waits on this.
