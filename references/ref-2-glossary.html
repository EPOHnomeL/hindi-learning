Current glossary

The words Jonathan can read so far, anchored in the BSI OV Bible (Psalm 1). A word is added only once it can be recognised in context. Revised in place as the vocabulary grows.

धन्य
dhanya — blessed, fortunate, happy. The opening word of Psalm 1’s blessing — क्या ही धन्य “how blessed!”.
पुरुष
puruṣ — man, person. Masculine noun. (Printed in the OV as पुरूष.)
जो
jo — who / which, the relative pronoun that opens a relative clause (“the man who walks…”). Pairs later with वह “that (one)”.
चलना
calnā — to walk, to move, to go along. Habitual: चलता है “walks”.
बैठना
baiṭhnā — to sit. Habitual: बैठता है “sits”.
होना
honā — to be / to become. The most important verb in Hindi; also the source of the है-auxiliary. खड़ा होना “to stand” (lit. “to become standing”).
नहीं
nahīṁ — not, the standard negator, placed before the verb. न (na) is its shorter, more literary twin, used for “and not / nor”.
परन्तु
parantu — but, however. The contrast hinge that opens Psalm 1:2 — turning from what the blessed man does not do (v1) to what he does (v2).
यहोवा
yahovā — the LORD, Yahweh — God’s covenant name (יהוה). The BSI OV uses it where many English Bibles print “the LORD”. A proper name; it never changes form. Appears in nearly every Psalm.
व्यवस्था
vyavasthā — law, instruction (the Torah). Feminine — so it takes की: यहोवा की व्यवस्था “the law of the LORD”.
प्रसन्न
prasanna — pleased, glad, delighted. With से: प्रसन्न … से “delighted in / pleased with”.
रहना
rahnā — to stay, remain, dwell; also the engine of the continuative करता रहता है “keeps on doing” (a habitual participle + रहना).
वृक्ष
vṛkṣ — tree. Masculine. The simile of Psalm 1:3: उस वृक्ष के समान “like that tree”.
समान
samān — like, similar, equal, alike (indeclinable). With possessive के it forms the compound postposition के समान “like / similar to”, which takes the oblique case.
सफल
saphal — successful, fruitful. सफल होता है “prospers / is successful” — the closing promise of Psalm 1:3.
दुष्ट
duṣṭ — wicked, evil (one). Oblique plural दुष्टों “of the wicked” (Psalm 1:1); as a plain plural subject, दुष्ट लोग “wicked people” (Psalm 1:4).
लोग
log — people, folk. A plural noun used to make a group subject: दुष्ट लोग “wicked people”. Pairs with the plural verb (होते हैं).
वे
ve — they / those. The plural of वह “he / that”. (Oblique form उन, as वह → उस.)
ऐसा
aisā — such, so, of this sort. Inflects like अच्छा: masc. pl. ऐसे. ऐसे नहीं होते “are not so” (Psalm 1:4).
भूसी
bhūsī — chaff, husk (feminine). The image of the wicked in Psalm 1:4: उस भूसी के समान “like that chaff”.
पवन
pavan — wind, breeze, air (masculine). पवन से उड़ाई जाती है “is blown away by the wind” — here से = “by”.
लगाना
lagānā — to plant, set, fix, apply. Passive लगाया जाना “to be planted”: लगाया गया है “has been planted” (Psalm 1:3, of the tree).
उड़ाना
uṛānā — to blow away, cause to fly, winnow. Passive उड़ाई जाना “to be blown away”: उड़ाई जाती है “is blown away” (Psalm 1:4, of the chaff).
जाना
jānā — to go. Beyond its plain sense, it is the passive auxiliary: a perfective participle + जाना means “to be …-ed” (लगाया गया, उड़ाई जाती).
इस कारण
is kāraṇ — for this reason, therefore. इस (oblique of यह “this”) + कारण “reason, cause” (masc.). The hinge that opens Psalm 1:5, looking back to vv.1–4.
अदालत
adālat — court of law, judgment (feminine). अदालत में “in the judgment” (Psalm 1:5).
स्थिर
sthir — firm, steady, stable (adjective). स्थिर रहना / होना “to stand firm, hold one’s ground” — स्थिर न रह सकेंगे “will not be able to stand firm” (Psalm 1:5).
सकना
saknā — to be able to, can. A helper placed after a bare verb stem: रह सकना “to be able to stay/stand”. It carries the tense ending — future रह सकेंगे “will be able to stand” (Psalm 1:5).
पापी
pāpī — sinner, one who sins (from पाप “sin”). Form unchanged in the plural: पापी … ठहरेंगे “sinners … will abide” (Psalm 1:5).
धर्मी
dharmī — righteous (one), godly person (from धर्म “righteousness, duty, religion”). Oblique plural धर्मियों: धर्मियों की मण्डली “the assembly of the righteous” (Psalm 1:5). Contrasts throughout the psalm with दुष्ट / पापी.
मण्डली
maṇḍalī — assembly, congregation, company (feminine) — hence की: धर्मियों की मण्डली “the congregation of the righteous” (Psalm 1:5).
ठहरना
ṭhaharnā — to stop, stay on, stand firm, abide (handbook p.115). Future ठहरेंगे “(they) will abide” — पापी … न ठहरेंगे “sinners will not abide” (Psalm 1:5).
