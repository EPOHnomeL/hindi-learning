# Hindi Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: *Teach Yourself Hindi* — Rupert Snell & Simon Weightman](./Handbook.pdf)
  The primary handbook (local `Handbook.pdf`, 181 pp., scanned). A trusted,
  widely-used course built around the "Kumar family" narrative. Ch. 1 (pp. 5–18)
  covers the Devanagari script and sound system; later chapters build grammar
  progressively (pronouns → gender → postpositions → tenses → relative clauses →
  passive). **Use for:** authoritative script, pronunciation, and grammar; every
  lesson should trace its claims back to a handbook page.
  Note: scanned images, no text layer — pages are read by rendering to PNG via
  `scripts/render_pages.py`.

- [BSI OV Hindi Bible — "Pavitra Bible" / Hindi Holy Bible (HHBD)](https://www.bible.com/bible/819/PSA.1.HHBD)
  The Bible Society of India "Old Version" — the target text for the whole
  mission. The classic literary Hindi rendering (uses यहोवा for the LORD).
  **Use for:** all reading passages, anchored vocabulary, and real-world practice.

- [Hindi Bible (parallel, multiple versions) — Sajeeva Vahini](https://hindibible.sajeevavahini.com/hindibible/index.aspx?b=19&c=1)
  Hindi Scripture with parallel English versions, verse by verse.
  **Use for:** cross-checking the OV against English when a verse is opaque.
